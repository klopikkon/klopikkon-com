About Us

Advanced Molecular Technologies (AMT) is a contract research organisation specialising in medicinal chemistry and custom organic synthesis. We synthesise target molecules, manufacture chemicals (small- and large-scale) and supply specialist chemicals around the world.

We further offer chemical method development, drug discovery, synthetic intermediates, building-blocks and finished-products to the pharmaceutical, biotech and chemical markets.

Advanced Molecular Technologies aims to be a biotech incubator and contract research organization specializing in medicinal chemistry and custom organic synthesis. Our services involve the design and synthesis of target molecules, structure activity correlations (SAR), the production of compounds under GLP suitable for in vivo studies, and analytical chemistry.

AMT is currently incubating organoboron pharmaceuticals and diagnostics, PEG linkers for antibody drug conjugates (ADCs) and AMPK activators.

Analytical Services

Our services extend towards the available use of laboratory-space, fume cupboards, equipment and analytical services. Analytical chemistry services include; Nuclear Magnetic Resonance (NMR), High Pressure Liquid Chromatography (HPLC), Gas Chromatography (GC), Infra-red (FT-IR), Flash Point, Optical Rotation, Dynamic Scanning Calorimetry (DSC), UV-Vis and Biotage Flash Chromatography. Expert analytical chemists are also available to interpret results, and consultation is offered upon request.
