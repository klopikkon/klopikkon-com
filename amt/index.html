Advanced Molecular Technologies against published catalogs

Three pages built from AMT’s current USD lists and competitor product pages. No invented prices. Where a vendor hides the number, the cell says quote only.

Page 1

AMT winning on price

Ionizable lipids and short PEGs where AMT’s published mg–g price is lower than Broadpharm, Cayman, Avanti, TCI or Sigma.

Page 2

AMT losing on price

Packs where a competitor prints a lower list price, or where AMT only sells a larger pack than the buyer needs.

Page 3

Unique, or a purity others do not list

Catalog items with no public equivalent, a higher stated grade, or an AMT-only high-purity SKU.

How to read the tables. Each row is one comparable pack. AMT price is the current published list. Competitor price links to the live product page. Catalog date is the AMT PDF or homepage capture used for the AMT number.

AMT homepage featured prices and the dated PDFs do not always match. Lipid numbers use the 26 Aug 2026 lipid PDF unless noted.
