Quote to invoice

The quote goes out. Then it sits. Then someone retypes it into an invoice.

How it works

If the bones are the same, that handoff is the job. We set it up so the email can carry both: here is the quote, and if you decide to proceed, here is the invoice, in the same message. You still check the numbers before anything is sent.

What this is not

Not a new accounting platform, or a promise that money collects itself.
