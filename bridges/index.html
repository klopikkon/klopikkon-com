03 · System Architecture & Field Survival 03 / 11

Engineered for Floods, Heatwaves, Frost, Wildlife & Theft

🌊 Flood Debris

Mounted in sheltered downstream girder pocket; logs deflect off upstream pier.

💧 Condensation

IP68 potted PCB + silicone O-ring + waterproof breather vent stops thermal breathing.

☀️ 46°C / Frost

Permanent under-deck shade + recycled HDPE/ASA + industrial -60°C to +85°C cell.

🐜 Ants & Wasps

Silicone-sealed rigid HDPE conduit beats rats/ants; flyscreen blocks mud wasps.

🔒 Rural Theft

No big solar panels or 12V batteries visible; ~$10 plastic antenna has zero resale value.

All external fittings clip to existing Victorian bridge width-marker or flood-depth signposts 03 / 11
04 · Subsystem Options — Structural & Flood Sensing 04 / 11

Modular Sensing Suite: Start Simple, Add What Each Bridge Needs

Every bridge starts with the sealed internal tilt & vibration core — then councils optionally plug in flood or crack sensors only on high-risk sites.

Core · Included Standard +$25–$40 BOM

Internal High-Res MEMS Tilt & Vibration

Industrial ADXL355 / SCL3300 rigidly bonded to the 4 mm enclosure floor — zero external sensor wires required.

Pier Scour & Settlement: Flags permanent tilt shifts down to ~0.1° after heavy rainfall.
Stiffness / Timber Rot Trend: Tracks gradual drops in natural vibration frequency as spans soften.
Overweight Truck Hits: Logs timestamped peak G-force shocks on load-limited rural crossings.
Pilot Prototype Option: $5–$8 MSA311 / MPU6050 for bench validation
Option S1 · Deck-Level Flood Float Switch +$8 AUD

Sealed magnetic float inside an upside-down recycled HDPE tube with stainless flyscreen & 2-min splash debounce. Records exact submersion start, end & duration — 100% immune to rain and mud wasps.

Option S2 · Under-Deck Ultrasonic Creek Gauge +$15 AUD

Waterproof ultrasonic transducer (JSN-SR04T) pointing down at the creek — measures continuous rising water height in centimetres before water reaches the girders.

Option S3 · Linear Crack Gauge & Future Timber Pucks +$25 / R&D

Optional gap sensor across a known crack. Thought experiment (R&D): tiny ~$40–$55 BLE “timber pucks” strapped to multiple beams for differential truck-flex & rot-moisture comparison back to 1 main box.

Output stays dead simple for engineers: clear threshold alerts (no noisy raw waveforms unless requested) 04 / 11
05 · Subsystem Options — Zero-Subscription Communications 05 / 11

One 915 MHz Radio Chip. Three Flexible Backhaul Options.

No need to stock separate bridge models — Australia’s licence-free 915 MHz ISM band runs both long-range live gateway pings and high-speed drive-by offloading on identical hardware.

Option C1 $0/mo per bridge

Shared Council LoRaWAN Gateway (Live)

Mount 1× $350–$500 outdoor LoRaWAN gateway on an existing council depot, town hall, or hilltop water tower (or reuse existing Northern Melbourne / AgVic gateways).

  • • Range: 10–15 km radius (10–30 bridges/gateway)
  • • Live Flood Alerts: Instant push within seconds
  • • Best For: Bridges within 15 km of towns/hills
Data rate: Slow / Long-Range LoRa
Option C2 $0/mo · No Gateway

Drive-By Data Muling (Ute / Drone)

Place an off-the-shelf ~$55–$70 dash-mounted 915 MHz receiver in existing council ranger/grader utes. As a ute crosses at 100 km/h, it scoops 3 months of logs (~120 KB) in under 2 seconds and auto-uploads via depot Wi-Fi.

  • • Range: 1–2 km curvy roads (5+ km flat)
  • • Flood Backup: Same radio talks to a drone or ute parked 1 km up the hill on dry ground
  • • Best For: Deep valleys & total blackspots
Data rate: High-Gear Fast LoRa / FSK
Option C3 · Recommended 1 Single SKU

Hybrid Auto-Fallback Firmware

Every bridge gets the exact same box. Firmware attempts a live gateway ping first; if out of range or blocked by terrain, it seamlessly queues logs for the next passing council ute or drone.

  • • Zero Inventory Split: Bolt any box to any bridge
  • • Optional Satellite Add-On: Adelaide’s Myriota nanosatellite (~$3–$5/mo) only on ultra-remote critical flood bridges
Best shire-wide operational simplicity
100 km/h Transfer Physics: A 1–2 km 915 MHz bubble gives a 35–70 sec window — 3 months of logs is only 32–200 KB (smaller than 1 phone photo).
Future Vision (Opt-In Crowd-Relay): Built-in BLE ($0 extra hardware) could eventually allow opt-in VicEmergency, Service Victoria, or local council apps on passing public phones to silently relay encrypted health/flood pings.
Uses Australia’s licence-free AU915 ISM spectrum (ACMA class-licensed — no radio licence fees) 05 / 11
06 · In-Vehicle Hardware & Software Architecture 06 / 11

Off-the-Shelf Dash Receiver & Self-Learning Triage Software

Zero custom car hardware to manufacture and zero driver clicks — paired with a lightweight anomaly engine that learns each bridge’s unique baseline.

Option V1 · Simplest Dash Mount ~$45–$70 AUD

Off-the-Shelf 915 MHz Pocket Box

Pre-cased Heltec V3/V4, LilyGO T-Beam, or RAK WisMesh Pocket placed on the dash (3M Velcro / non-slip mat) and plugged into the existing 12V USB port.

  • • 10-second setup: Zero roof wiring or door-seal cables
  • • ~500 m – 1.2 km through glass: 20–45 sec window at 100 km/h
  • • Auto Wi-Fi upload: Syncs automatically at depot (60s backup cell finishes upload after ignition off)
  • • Work-phone fallback: BLE handoff to council phone 4G
Option V2 · External Roof Puck (Optional) +$15 AUD

Magnetic 915 MHz roof puck only needed on steel-canopy trucks requiring maximum 2+ km range. (Early bench tests use an Android Chrome Web-Bluetooth page — $0 vehicle hardware.)

Fleet Upfront Example: 15 council utes × ~$60 = ~$900 one-off total
4-Layer Software Stack & Self-Learning Anomaly Engine
Priority-First (Newest-First) Transfer

Sends a 32-byte live flood/alert snapshot first (50 ms), then newest → oldest history — so even a brief 1–2 sec phone pass catches critical alerts.

30-Day Baseline & Triage (Zero Liability)

Each bridge learns its own tilt-vs-temperature curve & stiffness; flags abnormal shifts (>0.25° tilt / >7% stiffness drop) for engineers.

Deduplication by (bridge_id, seq) ensures multiple passing council vehicles never double-count records 06 / 11

11 · Proposed Zero-Risk Council Pilot Roadmap 11 / 11

Validate First, Scale Only When Proven in the Field

A three-phase rollout designed to require zero upfront budget and zero structural modification from a participating Victorian shire.

PHASE 1 · UNDERWAY Weeks 1–2

Roadside Bench & Drive-By Validation

  • • Verify clean 100 km/h drive-by wireless log transfer on open Victorian roads
  • • Bench-test rainproof/wasp-proof float switch & 2-min splash debounce
  • • 3D-print recycled rPETG v1 enclosure & verify IP68 seal
Council Commitment: $0 · Zero site access
PHASE 2 · PROPOSED PILOT 30 – 90 Days

Approved 1-Bridge Field Trial

  • • Council selects 1 low-risk rural creek bridge (ideally flood- or freight-prone)
  • • 5-minute zero-drill clamp-on install alongside council Assets team
  • • Validate real truck vibration logs, tilt drift, flood trigger & ute/gateway offload
Council Commitment: $0 · Non-invasive approval
PHASE 3 · SCALE Months 3–6+

Multi-Bridge Shire Rollout & Grants

  • • Transition enclosure to Melbourne recycled-HDPE injection moulding
  • • Deploy 10–30 priority flood/timber bridges (~$3.5k–$11k* all-in est.)
  • • ACMA AU915 (915–928 MHz) compliant; RCM certification path for commercial scale
  • • Co-fund via Sustainability Victoria / MAVlab grants
  • • Future Horizon: Opt-in public crowd-relay via VicEmergency / Service VIC / council apps
Target: ~$250–$400/bridge* installed · $0/mo ongoing
