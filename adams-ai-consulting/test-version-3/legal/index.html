Legal

I am an advisor. How you run your business is your decision. This page is how I work, not legal advice to you.

Get in touch

What this work is

I help small businesses look at their work and set up AI where it would save time. That can include sitting with a process, recommending a tool, and helping you get it running.

Online sessions may be recorded. The recording is sent as a download link that expires after seven days.

What this work is not

I am not your lawyer, your accountant, or your IT department. I do not run your business, approve what you send to customers, or take on the role of a director or employee. I do not guarantee a result.

AI tools and their output

AI models make mistakes. They can be wrong, biased, out of date, or invent things that are not true. A person in your business needs to check the work before it goes out.

Anything an AI tool produces in your business, including drafts, numbers, emails, quotes, invoices, and advice, sits with your company. I help you set the tools up. I am not responsible for what they do afterwards, or for any loss that follows.

Your decisions

You choose which tools to buy, what data to put in them, who can use them, and what you do with the output. Those choices, and their consequences for customers, staff, money, and the law, sit with you.

If a tool sends the wrong invoice, gives a bad quote, mishandles a customer, or leaks information, that is your company's matter. It does not come back to me as the person who helped you set it up.

Limits

To the extent Australian law allows, I am not liable for loss, damage, or claims arising from AI tools, their output, or decisions you make in the business after we have spoken.

Nothing on this page takes away rights you have under the Australian Consumer Law that cannot be excluded.
